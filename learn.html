Whale Species

Help Protect Whales

Whales face numerous threats in today's oceans, from climate change and pollution to ship strikes and entanglement in fishing gear. Many species are still recovering from the impacts of commercial whaling that lasted for centuries. Conservation efforts are crucial for maintaining healthy whale populations and preserving marine ecosystems. Whales play vital roles as ecosystem engineers, helping to maintain the balance of ocean food webs.

How kids can help:

Kids can make a big difference! Learning about whales, choosing reusable items, and keeping beaches clean are just a few ways to help the ocean. Every small action adds up!

  • Reduce Plastic Use: Use reusable water bottles and bags to keep plastic out of the ocean.
  • Beach Clean-ups: Join or organize beach clean-up events with your family.
  • Spread Awareness: Share what you learn about whales with friends and family.
  • Support Conservation: Learn about organizations protecting whales and their habitats.
