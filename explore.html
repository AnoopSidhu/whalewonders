Ocean Depth Zones

The Sunlight Zone (0–200m) is the brightest part of the ocean. Blue whales, humpbacks, dolphins, and more live here.

The Twilight Zone (200–1,000m) is dim and mysterious, home to deep-sea creatures, Sperm whales, and Beaked whales. Some species, like blue whales, will dive here to feed.

The Midnight Zone (1,000–4,000m) is pitch black with freezing temperatures and crushing pressure. While this zone is characterized by many invertebrates like squid, large whales are not known to inhabit it.
