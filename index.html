About Us:

Whale Wonders is a fun and educational space made for curious ocean explorers of all ages. Here, you can learn about different whale species, discover where they live, dive into the ocean zones, and play interactive activities that make marine science exciting and easy to understand. Whether you're looking at habitat maps, taking quizzes, or just exploring for fun, Whale Wonders is here to spark curiosity and inspire a love for our amazing oceans.
