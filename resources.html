Books

"The Whale" by Philip Hoare

A fascinating exploration of whales, human history, and marine life.

"Spying on Whales" by Nick Pyenson

Talks about how whales evolved, live, and survive from their ancient origins to the challenges they face in our changing oceans.

"Deep" by James Nestor

Explores the science of diving and the ocean’s hidden world.

Movies / Documentaries

"Blackfish" (2013)

A documentary about orcas in captivity and the ethical questions surrounding marine parks.

"Whale Rider" (2002)

A story about Maori culture and the spiritual connection to whales.

"Blue Planet II" (2017)

BBC documentary series exploring the wonders of the ocean and its inhabitants.
