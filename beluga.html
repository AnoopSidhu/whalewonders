Beluga Whale

Beluga whales (Delphinapterus leucas) are white whales native to Arctic and sub-Arctic waters. They are easily recognized by their rounded “melon” heads and lack of a dorsal fin. Belugas are highly social, traveling in pods and communicating with a wide range of clicks, whistles, and chirps. Their flexible necks allow them to turn their heads in all directions, and their thick blubber keeps them warm in freezing waters. Belugas feed on fish, crustaceans, and other small marine animals, and they form strong social bonds within their pods, often caring for calves together.
