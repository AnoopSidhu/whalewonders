Orca

Orcas, also known as killer whales (Orcinus orca), are the ocean’s ultimate predators and the largest members of the dolphin family. With their black-and-white coloring and dorsal fins, they’re unmistakable. These highly social animals travel in tight-knit family groups called pods, which can work together to hunt with incredible coordination. They use echolocation to navigate and locate prey. Orcas have a remarkably diverse diet: depending on the pod, they can feast on fish, squid, seabirds, seals, or even other whales. Lifespans vary, but many live to be 50–80 years old in the wild.
