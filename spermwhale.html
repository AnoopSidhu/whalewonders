Sperm Whale

Sperm whales (Physeter macrocephalus) are the largest toothed whales and are famous for their massive, square-shaped heads. They are deep divers, reaching over 3,000 meters to hunt squid, including the giant squid, making them some of the ocean’s most skilled predators. Sperm whales live in social groups called pods, often led by females, and communicate through a series of clicks known as codas. Found in oceans worldwide, these whales can live up to 70 years and are renowned for their intelligence, complex social behaviors, and long migrations.
